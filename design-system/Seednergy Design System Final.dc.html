Mobile design system · final · stone ground, lifted cards, cycle gauge

Warm stone ground at #EEECE7; every raised surface one step lighter at #F3F1EC, so layer one separates without leaving the one-material idea. One exception: text inputs sit inset on near-white, because a field must look like it receives. Each raised element carries a tight, hard bevel — bright inner edge top-left, warm inner edge bottom-right — so components read as domed rather than cut out. Cycle progress is never a bar: it is the logo's oval, filling, with the day count inside. Moment screens invert to forest and carry no tab bar.

Cards sit one step lighter than the ground: #F3F1EC on #EEECE7. Warm shadow down-right, near-white up-left. r1 for anything in a list, r2 for the one hero card per screen.

The bevel · hard edge on every raised element

Two inner shadows under the outer pair: inset 1.5px 1.5px 1px white top-left, inset -1.5px -1.5px 1px warm bottom-right — almost no blur, so the edge reads as a machined lip rather than a soft fade. Outer shadows are tight too: offset roughly equals blur. Applied to every raised element. OS chrome — status bar, keyboard — never receives it.

Division of labour
Terracotta deep — every screen title in Crimson, the active tab, and the second layer: any panel nested inside a card takes it with stone type. The strongest signal in the app, reserved for the coach's voice and the next action.
Olive — the one primary button per screen, the brand mark, the completion badge, and the “new” status.
Sage — growth only: the cycle-gauge fill and stage labels. Never a button, so action and progress never look alike.
Forest — the dark anchor: inverted grounds, “on track”, and every named item in a list.
Alert clay — “needs check” and nothing else. Its scarcity is what makes it work.
Ink — all body and label type, and inactive tab icons; never a card background.
Warm sand — photo mats and the tab bar's own ground.

Reading the library
P4 (home), P5 (grouped list), P6 (detail) and P1-inverted (moment) are the five screens in section 05. The eleven here complete the set. P1 alone carries 22 of the 75 screens, P6 carries 12 — build those two well and most of the app follows. Every screen above is composed only from the tokens in sections 02–04; nothing here introduces a value that isn't in handoff/tokens.css. Note P9: the keyboard is system chrome and is deliberately flat — app relief stops at the app's own surfaces. P2: SSO buttons carry olive labels but stay unfilled, so the screen keeps exactly one primary. And P14: rank progress stays a bar, because a rank is not a cycle — the oval gauge is reserved for growth toward harvest, and spending it on points would dilute the one place it means something.
07 — Handoff notes

Ship relief as two tokens

Two tokens compose every surface: shadow.raised (+ .lg for hero cards) and shadow.bevel, always applied together. Colour tokens stay semantic: canvas, sand, action, accent, ink — plus an inverted set for moment screens.

Watch in build

Bevelled relief needs four shadows per element (two outer, two inner) with tight blur radii — platform elevation APIs can't express it, so cast it as layered shadows. Keep 14px minimum between raised elements.

Still open

Real photography, the remaining seven screens (Explore, seed detail, Garden, the three paywalls — the paywalls take the inverted ground), dark mode, and empty/error states.
